What’s inside this simulation?
256 local, seeded synthetic windows contain temperature, vibration, and current. Exactly 32 are labeled anomalies. The field-noise fixture adds known benign motor-start bursts; the dropout fixture also removes 9 vibration readings. Missing windows are excluded from every metric denominator, never silently filled.
Both profiles use fixed formulas, not trained or hardware-benchmarked models. Latency = 0.05 + 0.003 × operations (ms). Energy = 18 + 0.06 × operations + 0.002 × memory bytes (µJ). Battery assumes 4.44 Wh and includes sensor acquisition and inference only — not radio, idle power, peripherals, or battery losses. Threshold recommendations are fitted to this fixture, not independent validation.
Deployment cards record the scenario, seed, threshold, parameters, fixture fingerprint, cost formulas, and coverage. The displayed FNV-1a fingerprint covers the compact JSON body, before its checksum is added. It is a repeatability check, not a cryptographic signature.
