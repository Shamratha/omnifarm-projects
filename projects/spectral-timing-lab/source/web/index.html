i
Conventions used in this workbench

Signals must be uniformly sampled on the same time grid. Defaults are a periodic Hann window, per-segment mean subtraction, mean averaging, and one-sided density normalization by sample rate × sum(window²). Spectrum normalization divides by sum(window)². Complete segments use 50% overlap; incomplete tails are discarded. NaN omission drops a segment from both channels without removing samples or interpolating. Rejection includes NaNs in the incomplete tail of the analyzed prefix. Truncation uses the aligned common prefix while validating both full time grids. Median averaging is uncorrected and uses separate real/imaginary cross medians. Cross spectrum is conj(FFT(A)) × FFT(B); phase is wrapped to [−π, π].
